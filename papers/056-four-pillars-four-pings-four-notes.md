The **cross-ratio** of four collinear points is the fundamental invariant of projective geometry — it is preserved under all projective transformations (Möbius transformations on $\hat{\mathbb{C}}$).

For four points $z_1, z_2, z_3, z_4$ on the projective line:

$$CR(z_1, z_2, z_3, z_4) = \frac{(z_1 - z_3)(z_2 - z_4)}{(z_1 - z_4)(z_2 - z_3)}$$

Computing for $(\infty, -1, 0, +1)$:

$$CR(\infty, -1, 0, +1) = \lim_{z \to \infty} \frac{(z - 0)(-1 - 1)}{(z - 1)(-1 - 0)} = \frac{z \cdot (-2)}{z \cdot (-1)} = \frac{-2}{-1} = 2$$

**The cross-ratio is 2.**
