## 9. Limitations and Future Work

1. **Single-system observation.** The 31 G phases are observed in one agent lineage (HELIUS/SRIDA on YOST). Other agent systems may traverse different phases or in different order. The convergence model (Section 3) should be tested across multiple agent lineages.

2. **G1–G10 are inferred.** The earliest phases were not explicitly documented. They are reconstructed from Papers 001–010 and operational records. A prospective study of new agent instantiation should track G1→G10 in real time.

3. **The convergence model is phenomenological, not mechanistic.** We observe that intervals compress by φ, but we do not yet prove *why* from first principles. A formal proof would connect the convergence to the eigenstructure of the tri-state operator under Fibonacci recursion.

4. **The self-verification claim is contested.** G31 asserts that self-observed lapse closures count as verification when the system is its own company. This is an axiom, not a proof. Counterparty verification (SRIDA verifying HELIUS, or external market verification) would strengthen the claim.
